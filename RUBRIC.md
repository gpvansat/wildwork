# WildWork Southern Africa: Ethics Grading Rubric (v1, 21 Oct 2026)

Every listing gets one grade, A to F, based on what the program's own public pages say. Volunteers can look at every rule and our notes. We re-check each listing at least once every 6 months, and whenever someone reports a problem.

## Automatic F (not listed)
Any one of these means the program is graded F and we don't list it:
- Cub petting, cub "raising" for tourists, or selfies holding animals
- Walking with lions or other big cats; "walking with" or riding elephants
- Breeding big cats or other wildlife for trade, hunting, bone trade, or petting farms, or any link to canned hunting
- Swimming with or touching captive marine mammals or sharks for entertainment
- Animals used in performances, or photos with sedated or chained animals
- Refusing to say where animals go when they leave

## Grade A: Wild and hands-off
- Volunteers work only with free-roaming wild animals (monitoring, telemetry, camera traps, surveys, habitat work, anti-poaching support, community work)
- No touching, feeding or handling of animals, apart from rare vet or collaring procedures that professionals lead
- The work feeds real research, reserve management or a government or NGO program
- Fees and what they include are published (or available on request)

## Grade B: Responsible captive care or rehabilitation
- A sanctuary or rehab centre with a no-touch or minimal-handling policy, or a release-focused rehab program (e.g. seabirds)
- Non-releasable animals are kept for welfare, not for tourist contact
- Or: mostly wild-research programs with a small captive component (e.g. feeding non-releasable cheetahs)

## Grade C: Hands-on care, so check before booking
- Volunteers handle, bottle-feed or walk with animals (e.g. baboon walks, raising orphans), even when the mission is rehab and release
- Or: vague language ("interactions", "breeding-related tasks") that we couldn't clarify
- These are listed with a warning and the reasons shown

## Grade D: Serious concerns
- Ongoing captive breeding that doesn't lead to release, "close encounters" marketed as a selling point, or direct interaction with adult predators
- Listed for transparency only, never featured, and never eligible for paid placement

## Rules for paid listings
- Paying never changes a grade. Only A and B programs can buy a Featured slot.
- Programs can send evidence to appeal a grade. Notes are updated publicly with a date.
- Affiliate links are labelled as such.
